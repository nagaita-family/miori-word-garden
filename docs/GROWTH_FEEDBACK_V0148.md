
# v0.14.8 Growth Feedback Update

Functional baseline remains v0.14.6; v0.14.7 added persistent Garden XP and the Growth Path.

## Goal
Make XP growth visible at the exact moment an activity earns it, without forcing the page to scroll away from the Garden.

## Feedback sequence
1. Activity completes and keeps its existing reward (watering, seed, sun, harvest, present, etc.).
2. A fixed overlay appears above the current screen with `+XP` and the current Garden Level.
3. The XP bar visibly animates from the old value to the new value while an energy sparkle flies across it.
4. If the bar crosses a level boundary, it fills to 100%, flashes, rolls into the new level, and fills to the new remainder.
5. A larger `GARDEN LEVEL UP!` celebration follows with stronger fanfare, sparkles, and confetti.

Hint and Peek never reduce XP.
